## Brand & Style

The visual identity embodies the dignity, resilience, and operational precision of an artisanal cooperative federation. Moving decisively away from frictionless, synthetic corporate SaaS templates—no synthetic violets, no neon gradients, and no generic tech-blue hues—this design system is rooted in the tangible world of craft, physical ledgers, guildhalls, and communal trade. 

The aesthetic marries **editorial archival structure** with **high-density administrative utility**. It evokes the gravitas of a master craftsman's workshop register or an agricultural union ledger: authoritative, grounded, tactile, and permanent. Typography balances the intellectual warmth of literary serifs with the pragmatic legibility of humanist grotesque sans-serifs. Surfaces favor rich, warm cream papers and physical borders over floaty drop shadows. 

Every view should evoke transparency, stewardship, collective solidarity, and institutional dependability for cooperative admins, field coordinators, and federation leadership.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from raw natural materials: terracotta clay, scorched earth, olive groves, and handmade archival vellum.

### Roles & Applications
- **Primary (`#B45309` - Terracotta):** Represents core administrative actions, focused states, interactive badges, table highlights, and navigational anchors. It anchors primary workflows with an earthy, baked-clay warmth.
- **Secondary (`#C2410C` - Rust Red):** Dedicated to high-priority alerts, urgent cooperative actions, grievance flagging, critical deficits, and SOS interventions. It carries an organic urgency without resembling synthetic danger sirens.
- **Tertiary (`#4D7C0F` - Olive Green):** Communicates operational vitality, quota compliance, verified cooperative standing, active health statuses, and positive yields.
- **Neutral (`#FAF6F0` - Warm Cream Canvas):** Acts as the foundational substrate, mimicking heavy cotton rag paper. Sub-surfaces scale into `#F4ECE1` for elevated panels and `#ECE2D0` for nested sub-containers.
- **Structural Lines (`#E5DDD0` & `#D6C7B2`):** Crisp, warm-tinted hairline borders that partition dense data fields without relying on blurry visual depth.
- **Text & Ink (`#1C1917` & `#292524` - Dark Charcoal / Stone Black):** High-contrast, tactile ink tones replacing cold pure black for all legibility-critical information and data rows. Muted body tones utilize `#57534E`.

## Typography

The type system creates an intentional dual rhythm:
- **Literata** handles key ledger statistics, federation report titles, section headings, and primary KPI metrics. Its bookish, authoritative cadence gives administrative dashboards the presence of a formal ledger of records.
- **Karla** provides functional, compact, and highly legible clarity for complex tables, nested menus, form inputs, status tags, and high-density metric labels.

### Rules of Usage
1. Use uppercase for `label-sm` and `label-md` when rendering metadata, cooperative unit IDs, status chips, or table headers.
2. Tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) must be active for all tables, currency indicators, and KPI readouts to preserve column alignment across recurring numbers.

## Layout & Spacing

The dashboard employs a structured **hybrid column grid** built on tight, rhythmic increments suited for data-dense cooperative management.

### Grid Anatomy & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px and up):** 12-column layout with `2rem` page margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Admin panels, metrics cards, and data matrices sit alongside a persistent, structured left navigation rail (fixed width: `260px`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Navigation collapses to an icon bar or drawer.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and `0.75rem` gutters. Multicolumn data tables convert into vertically stacked card records.

### Spacing Philosophy
Spacing reflects the disciplined discipline of ledger books: dense, neatly gridded, and cleanly segmented. Generous padding is reserved strictly for macro structural headers, while table rows and control fields remain compact and actionable.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids floating, synthetic drop shadows or glassy blurs. Depth is produced through **crisp architectural borders**, **tonal surface stepping**, and **structural inset grouping**.

### Spatial Techniques
1. **Hairline Outlines:** Containers and cards rely on a 1px solid border (`#E5DDD0`) on baseline surfaces, sharpening to `#D6C7B2` on focused or interactive states.
2. **Tonal Tiering:**
   - **Canvas (Ground):** `#FAF6F0` (base parchment).
   - **Cards & Modules:** `#FFFFFF` (pure white sheet overlay) or `#F5EFEB` for secondary modules.
   - **Well / Recessed Areas:** `#EDE5D8` for nested code blocks, batch audit logs, or input fields.
3. **Restrained Hover Shadows:** When an element requires lift (such as a dropdown menu or modal sheet), use an opaque, tight, architectural offset shadow: `0px 2px 0px 0px #D6C7B2`, avoiding blurry ambient spreads.
4. **Dividers:** Use explicit 1px horizontal and vertical rules rather than empty spatial separation to keep data readable across expansive displays.

## Shapes

The shape language uses soft, measured corner radii (`0.25rem` base, `0.5rem` for cards and modals) that soften the visual field without becoming overly rounded or play-like.

- **Base Controls:** Inputs, buttons, and row entries use `0.25rem` (`rounded-sm` / `rounded-md`) to mirror trimmed paper slips.
- **Icon Buttons:** Use balanced `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`) squircle containers to give tactile affordance to utility triggers.
- **Data Cards:** Standardized to `0.5rem` corner radius with crisp 1px borders.
- **Status Badges & Pills:** Maintain `0.25rem` to avoid circular bubble shapes, keeping the system aligned with industrial federation tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#B45309`, text `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#92400E`. Hover shifts to `#92400E`.
- **Secondary (Urgent / SOS):** Background `#C2410C`, text `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#9A3412`. Used exclusively for critical calls to action, urgent dispatches, or conflict resolutions.
- **Inverted (Authoritative):** Background `#1C1917`, text `#FAF6F0`, border 1px solid `#1C1917`. Ideal for administrative batch commits and report exports.
- **Outlined:** Background `transparent`, text `#292524`, border 1px solid `#D6C7B2`. Hover shifts background to `#F5EFEB`.
- **Shape & Sizing:** Height 36px (compact) or 42px (default), font family Karla, font weight 700, radius 4px (`rounded-sm`).

### Badges & Status Chips
- **Active / Healthy:** Olive green tint (Background `#F2F7EC`, text `#365314`, border 1px solid `#84CC16`).
- **Pending / Action:** Terracotta tint (Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#92400E`, border 1px solid `#F59E0B`).
- **SOS / Urgent:** Rust red tint (Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, border 1px solid `#EF4444`).
- Chips remain rectangular with `0.25rem` radius, tracking uppercase lettering (`label-sm`).

### Form Inputs & Fields
- **Container:** Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid border `#D6C7B2`, radius 4px.
- **Typography:** Karla 14px text in `#1C1917`. Placeholder `#78716C`.
- **Focus State:** 1px solid `#B45309` with a crisp 1px outline offset in `#B45309`, never a soft glow.
- **Labels:** Positioned above inputs using Karla Bold 12px with uppercase tracking (`#44403C`).

### Cards & Ledger Tables
- **Cards:** White canvas surface (`#FFFFFF`), outlined with 1px `#E5DDD0`, internal padding of `space-lg` (`1.25rem`). Card headers feature `headline-sm` Literata typography with an optional subtle hairline border underneath.
- **Ledger Tables:** Strict baseline formatting. Header rows use `#F5EFEB` with uppercase Karla 11px text. Alternating rows remain white or soft cream (`#FAF6F0`). Row hover uses `#F4ECE1`. Data borders are 1px solid `#E5DDD0`.

### Cooperative Federation Specifics
- **Artisan Unit Summary Bar:** A dense horizontal bar showing active guild members, operational clusters, pending grievance count, and collective vault balance. Metrics use Literata display typography with Karla sub-labels.
- **Emergency Dispatch Banner:** Rust-red (`#C2410C`) top banner with white typography and inverted cream button for urgent federation-wide broadcast alerts.
